Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What this app does

Career Intelligence analyses resume and job description text you provide, and shows match scores, skill gaps and a draft cover letter.

Data we handle

  • Text you paste or load (resume, job description). It is sent to an AI service (Anthropic) to produce the analysis.
  • Saved on your device only: your last resume text, saved jobs, theme and settings, stored in your browser's local storage.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no login, no ads, no analytics.
  • We do not sell your data or store your resume on our servers.

Third parties

Text you submit is processed by Anthropic to generate results, under its terms and privacy policy. The app is hosted on Netlify.

Your choices

Clear your browser or app storage to delete everything saved on your device. Do not include sensitive information you do not want processed.

AI limits

Scores and suggestions are AI estimates and may be wrong. They do not guarantee interviews or job offers.
